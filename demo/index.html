How Hyperliquid's best traders are positioned

The open perp positions of the top wallets, added up by coin: how many are long and how many short, how much on each side, their average entry and leverage, and how much they are up. Every large trade on the busiest coins as it happens, with a star when it comes from a trader on the board. Open any wallet for its positions, its profit over the month, its record and its fills. Everything comes from Hyperliquid's public API, with no key and no account. Free and MIT licensed.
